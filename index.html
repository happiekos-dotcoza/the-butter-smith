The Story Behind The Butter Smith

At The Butter Smith, we believe butter isn't just an ingredient—it’s the heart of every great gourmet dish. Founded on traditional churning methods and a drive for unexpected flavor combinations, our kitchen creates small batches of garlic herb, sweet honey, and spicy compound butters.

Alongside our signature butter logs, we prepare artisanal dried fruit relishes, sweet crunchy barks, and rich traditional preserves—perfect for entertaining boards, savory steaks, freshly baked artisan breads, and sweet morning treats.
