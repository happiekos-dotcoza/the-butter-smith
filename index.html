Legal Notice & Compliance

Privacy Notice & POPIA Compliance

At The Butter Smith, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information in full compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (Act 4 of 2013) ("POPIA") of South Africa.

1. Information We Collect

When you register an account, update your shipping details, or place an order, we process necessary personal information including your full name, email address, contact phone number, and physical delivery address.

2. Purpose of Collection & Usage

Your information is collected strictly for fulfilling artisanal orders, arranging local insulated delivery in Centurion and Gauteng, maintaining customer accounts, and communicating essential order updates.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement secure encrypted cloud data storage (via Google Firebase) and standard safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

4. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask for deletion of your stored details at any time by contacting us at happiekos@gmail.com.
