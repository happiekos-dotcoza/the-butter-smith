Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Quick answers regarding delivery, store collection, and artisanal product care.

Where can I buy compound butter in Centurion?

You can order handcrafted compound butters directly online from The Butter Smith Deli for local delivery across Centurion, Pretoria, and Midrand, or collect your order for free at our weekend market stalls including Bus Stop 7 Open Air Market.

What are the local delivery areas & fees?

We offer fresh, local delivery within a 30km radius of Rooihuiskraal, Centurion. Delivery fees are dynamically calculated at R3.50 per kilometer on the round trip from our prep base.

Can non-perishables be shipped via courier?

Yes! While fresh perishable items (like compound butters, brownies, and blondies) are restricted to local fresh delivery within 30km or store pickup, all non-perishable products (like our dried fruit relishes and barks) can be shipped anywhere in South Africa via nationwide courier for a flat rate of R99.

Where & when can I collect for free?

Free collection is available at two pickup points: Rooihuiskraal Pickup Base on weekdays (09:00 - 16:00), or Bus Stop 7 Open Air Market on Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays.

How should I store compound butter?

Keep your compound butter refrigerated between 2°C and 5°C. It stays fresh in the fridge for up to 3 weeks or can be frozen for up to 6 months without losing flavor or quality.

Is there a first-time buyer discount?

Yes! First-time registered customers automatically receive a 5% discount applied to their cart subtotal on their very first order. Minimum order requirement is R100.00.

Legal Notice & Compliance

Privacy Notice & POPIA Compliance

At The Butter Smith Deli, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect personal information necessary to process orders, fulfill local or courier deliveries, and provide customer support. This includes your full name, cell phone number, email address, physical delivery address, and order transaction history.

2. PURPOSE OF DATA PROCESSING

Your personal information is collected and processed solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Processing, packing, and dispatching your artisanal butter and small-batch orders.
  • Calculating exact local delivery distances and courier shipping costs.
  • Communicating order status updates via email or WhatsApp.
  • Managing registered customer accounts and order history.

3. DATA STORAGE & SECURITY

All client records and transaction data are securely stored using encrypted cloud services powered by Firebase. We implement strict security measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or misuse of your personal data.

4. THIRD-PARTY SHARING

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Data is shared exclusively with necessary service providers required to fulfill your order (e.g., payment gateways like Yoco, or delivery/courier services).

5. YOUR RIGHTS UNDER POPIA

You have the right to request access to your personal information, request corrections, or request deletion of your account and personal records at any time by contacting our Information Officer at happiekos@gmail.com.

Store Policy & Terms

Refund, Shipping & Return Policy

By placing an order on our website, you agree to our Shipping, Delivery, and Return Policies outlined below.

1. PERISHABLE GOODS & FINAL SALES

Due to health regulations, food safety standards, and the fresh, perishable nature of our handcrafted compound butters and small-batch bakes, all sales are final once orders have been prepared or dispatched. Returns or exchanges of food items are strictly prohibited once they have left our prep facility.

2. DAMAGED OR INCORRECT ITEMS

If you receive an incorrect item or an item that has been physically damaged during local delivery, please notify us within 24 hours of receipt by emailing happiekos@gmail.com or WhatsApping us with photographs of the item and packaging. Verified damaged or incorrect orders will be replaced promptly or issued a store credit.

3. ORDER CANCELLATIONS

Orders may be cancelled for a full refund prior to order preparation or dispatch. Once an order is marked as "In Prep" or "Processing" in our system, cancellations can no longer be accepted as raw ingredients will have already been allocated.

4. UNCOLLECTED MARKET & STORE ORDERS

Orders selected for free store or weekend market pickup (Bus Stop 7 / Rooihuiskraal) must be collected during designated market operating hours. Uncollected perishable items cannot be refunded after the scheduled pickup window expires.

Delivery Guidelines

Shipping & Distance Policy

1. LOCAL FRESH DELIVERY RATE & RADIUS

Local fresh deliveries originate from our main prep base at 153 Bokmakierie Road, Rooihuiskraal, Centurion.

Local delivery is dynamically calculated at a rate of R3.50 per kilometer on the round trip from our location to your specified delivery address.

Fresh local delivery is strictly capped at a maximum distance radius of 30km (one-way) to maintain cold-chain integrity and product quality.

2. RESTRICTED PERISHABLE PRODUCTS

Compound Butters, Brownies, and Fresh Bakes are temperature-sensitive perishable products. They are strictly restricted to local fresh delivery within our 30km radius or for store/market collection.

Orders containing perishable goods that exceed the 30km local delivery radius will require free collection or removal of perishable items before checkout can proceed.

3. NATIONWIDE COURIER SHIPPING (NON-PERISHABLES)

Non-perishable items (such as our Dried Fruit Relishes, Barks, and dry goods) are eligible for nationwide courier delivery anywhere in South Africa for a flat rate of R99.00.

4. FREE COLLECTION OPTIONS & LOCATIONS

We offer free pickup at two designated collection locations:

  • Rooihuiskraal Prep Base: Weekdays (Monday to Friday) from 09:00 to 16:00.
  • Bus Stop 7 Open Air Market: Saturdays, Sundays, and Public Holidays during market trading hours.

5. MINIMUM ORDER VALUE

A minimum cart value of R100.00 applies to all online checkout orders across all delivery and collection options.
